How to Play?
Controls
W / S Thrust forward / backwards
A / D Rotate left / right
SPACE / SHIFT Vertical thrust (lift off!)
R Lose one life & reset to spawn
C Toggle CRT/retro look
Enter Restart after GAME OVER
The OBJECTIVE
  • Follow the green arrow to a pickup platform and land softly to pick up a passenger.
  • The arrow switches to yellow and points to the dropoff platform, land there to deliver your passenger.
  • Your fare counts down over time, get to your destination quick for a higher payout!
  • Land on blue pads to refuel.
  • Crashing costs a life and resets you to spawn.
  • Every few deliveries, you advance to a new level. Good pilots can earn extra lives.
Press C to turn the full CRT/retro mode on or off.
